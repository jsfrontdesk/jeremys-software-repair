FAQ

Is my data private?

Yes. I only access what's needed for the fix, ask your permission before touching personal files, and recommend a backup before any major work.

What if it's a hardware problem?

I focus on software. If the issue turns out to be hardware (a cracked screen, failed battery or bad drive), I'll tell you and suggest next steps.

How do I pay?

Payment is due when the job is done. Ask about accepted payment methods when you book.

Do you offer password help for any account?

Only for devices and accounts that belong to you.
